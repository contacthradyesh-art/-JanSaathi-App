# Monetization Plan

## Free Tier
- Scheme search
- Eligibility check
- Document guide
- Nearby center locator (basic)

## Paid Tier (Premium)
- Expert consultation (chat/call)
- Form filling assistance
- Priority support
- Application tracking with notifications

## B2B Model
- CSC centers/NGOs ko app white-label karke dena
- Bulk licensing for community organizations

## Government Partnership
- Official channel banne ka scope
- Government data/API integration ke through credibility

## Revenue Streams Summary
| Stream | Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Premium subscription | B2C recurring | High |
| Expert consultation fee | Per-use | High |
| CSC/NGO partnership commission | B2B | Medium |
| White-label licensing | B2B | Medium |
| Government partnership | Grant/Contract | Long-term |
