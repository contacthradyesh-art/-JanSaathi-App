# Problem Statement

## Core Problem
Log government schemes, certificates, RTI process ko samajh nahi paate — language barrier, complex process, aur jaankari ki kami ki wajah se agents ko extra paisa dete hain ya scheme se miss out kar jate hain.

## Why This Matters
- Karodon logo tak sahi jaankari nahi pahunchti
- Bichauliye (middlemen/agents) galat guide karte hain ya zyada paisa lete hain
- Forms complex hote hain, regional language me available nahi hote
- Low literacy users ke liye process samajhna mushkil hai

## Opportunity
- Clear paying customers (Expert help, form filling assistance)
- Government partnership ka scope
- Social impact + business dono possible
- Existing solutions (agents, cyber cafes) inefficient aur costly hain
